01 / AI & FULL STACK

Synapse Solution

A real-time agentic AI dashboard that unifies Finance, Sales, and Operations workflows.

Stack: Next.js · React · TypeScript · WebSockets/SSE · Zustand

01

Goal / Background

Finance, Sales, and Operations often work across disconnected tools, creating duplicate updates and slow decisions. Synapse Solutions was developed for the IBM x UTD case competition to explore a unified agentic AI collaboration hub.

02

Implementation

Designed a cross-functional dashboard, AI-assisted updates, smart alerts, an interactive organization view, and scenario planning. The portfolio lists Next.js, React, TypeScript, WebSockets/SSE, and Zustand; the competition deck also proposes Node.js, PostgreSQL, and IBM watsonx integrations.

03

Your Role

Co-created the case competition solution with Hajar Abdulkadir. The project materials document the joint proposal, interface demonstrations, and technical architecture; a feature-by-feature division of ownership is not documented.

04

Impact / Numbers

The portfolio describes dashboard workflows designed for 14 users. The competition proposed targets of 20% faster decisions and 30% less duplicate work; these are projected benefits, not measured deployment outcomes.

05

Tradeoffs / Alternatives

WebSockets versus Server-Sent Events is an important design choice for dashboard updates. Two-way events favor WebSockets, while server-to-client notifications can use SSE; the materials do not establish a benchmarked final selection.

06

Optimizations

A next iteration could add authenticated role-based access, reliable integration retries, audit logging, event delivery guarantees, and usability testing with actual cross-functional teams.

02 / DATA PLATFORM

DemandSync

A forecasting and ETL platform that turns transaction data into actionable inventory insights.

Stack: Python · Flask · PostgreSQL · dbt · pytest · XGBoost

01

Goal / Background

Inventory teams need to forecast demand, identify possible shortages, and make more informed procurement decisions. DemandSync explores how a dashboard and predictive models can turn supply-chain information into action.

02

Implementation

Built an inventory and forecasting workflow with APIs, database-backed data, and dashboard views. The team presentation shows React, Tailwind CSS, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, and Prophet using mock demonstration data; the portfolio also describes Flask, dbt, pytest, and XGBoost work.

03

Your Role

Listed as team lead in the DemandSync presentation, coordinating a multidisciplinary project. The deck identifies separate frontend, API, full-stack, and model contributors; it does not give a complete task-by-task breakdown of individual ownership.

04

Impact / Numbers

The existing portfolio documents 150,000+ processed transactions, eight REST endpoints, 25+ automated tests, and 5.2% forecasting MAPE. The uploaded presentation notes that its demonstration dataset is synthetic rather than live business data.

05

Tradeoffs / Alternatives

Prophet offers interpretable trend and seasonality modeling; the portfolio also references XGBoost. The supplied materials do not document a controlled comparison between those approaches, so no winning-model claim is made.

06

Optimizations

Add monitored forecasting drift, retraining, more robust API and data-quality checks, and real data integrations. The team roadmap proposes restaurant traffic signals and direct procurement connections.

03 / FINANCIAL DATA

Financial Disclosure Pipeline

An automated pipeline that structures SEC annual filings for analysis and validation.

Stack: Python · SQL · ETL · SEC filings · Validation

01

Goal / Background

Annual SEC filings are lengthy and inconsistent, making financial metrics difficult to compare and validate at scale. This pipeline structures disclosures into records suitable for downstream analysis.

02

Implementation

Used Python and SQL in an ETL workflow to ingest 10-K documents, extract structured metrics, validate schemas, and prepare analysis-ready outputs. The emphasis is repeatable data transformation and quality controls.

03

Your Role

Developed the filing-processing and validation workflow described in the portfolio. The available project summary does not specify collaborators or assign individual subtasks beyond that work.

04

Impact / Numbers

The portfolio reports 800+ SEC 10-K filings ingested, 50+ earnings metrics produced, 30+ schema and quality checks, and 500,000+ data points processed.

05

Tradeoffs / Alternatives

Strict validation can protect downstream analytics but may require extra handling for irregular filings. The available notes do not document a formal comparison against permissive ingestion or alternative parsing methods.

06

Optimizations

Expand coverage for unusual filing layouts, add lineage and extraction-confidence tracking, monitor quality failures, and use incremental processing to reduce repeated work.
