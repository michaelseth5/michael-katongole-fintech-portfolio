Synapse Solutions — Agentic AI for Cross-Department Collaboration.

September 2025 · Prepared by Hajar Abdulkadir and Michael Katongole.

02 / 10
CASE COMPETITION

The problem

Finance, Sales, Operations and other teams often operate in separate systems. Duplicated work, delayed handoffs and limited visibility can slow decisions.

Finance silo+Sales silo+Operations silo
03 / 10
CASE COMPETITION

The proposed solution

A unified agentic AI collaboration hub connecting ERP, CRM and project tools. The proposal brings status, reporting and cross-functional task coordination into one interface.

ERP & CRM→Synapse AI Hub→Shared dashboard
04 / 10
CASE COMPETITION

Product capabilities

  • Unified data dashboard for cross-department visibility
  • Smart alerts for duplicate work and budget conflicts
  • Scenario planning across Finance, Sales and Operations
  • Automated cross-functional reporting
  • AI assistant for task updates and collaboration

How the assistant works

1. Natural-language input→2. AI extracts structured data→3. Multi-system sync→4. Team notifications

07 / 10
CASE COMPETITION

Proposed technical architecture

  • Frontend: Next.js, React, Tailwind CSS, interactive organization charts
  • Backend: Node.js / Express and relational data storage
  • Data: event pipelines and cloud storage concepts
  • AI: IBM watsonx and transformer-based task understanding

This is the proposed architecture described in the competition, not a verification of each integration's deployment.
